Yujie Zhao

I am currently a second-year master student at VIPL Group, Institute of Computing Technology (ICT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), supervised by Prof. Shiguang Shan and Prof. Jiabei Zeng. Prior to that, I recieved my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) in 2024.

I am currently also a research intern at PKU-AgiBot Lab, supervised by Prof. Hao Dong. Prior to that, I had a good time at Harvard Visual Computing Group working with Prof. Hanspeter Pfister and Dr. Wanhua Li.

My research interests mainly include 3D vision, multi-modal models and embodied intelligence.
